This Warranty & Returns Policy (“Policy”) applies to purchases and services from Shadow Technologies Ltd (“Shadow”, “we”, “us”). It should be read with our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2) Exclusions

  • Accidental damage, impact, crushing, bending, or liquid ingress.
  • Software issues, malware, or data loss; user configuration errors.
  • Wear-and-tear (including natural battery capacity loss over time).
  • Unauthorised opening, modification, or third-party repair.
  • Security/state changes: warranty is void if the device is rooted, bootloader unlocked, or device-security flags are tripped (e.g., Knox tripped).
  • Cosmetic issues disclosed at sale (scratches, scuffs, minor marks).
  • Water resistance after any repair is not guaranteed unless explicitly stated in writing.

3) What we will do

If a covered defect is confirmed during the warranty period, we will, at our discretion:

  • Repair the product using OEM parts where available; or
  • Replace with an equivalent grade/spec device; or
  • Refund the purchase price if repair or replacement is not feasible.

4) Your responsibilities

  • Back up your data before booking a repair or returning a device. We are not responsible for data loss.
  • Provide proof of purchase and a clear fault description (photos/video where possible).
  • Return the device in safe packaging with all required accessories if requested.

5) How to make a claim

Email contact@shadowtechnologies.eu with:

  • Order number or invoice, IMEI/Serial, model and capacity.
  • Detailed description of the issue and steps to reproduce.
  • Photos or a short video illustrating the fault.
  • Return address and contact phone (for courier updates).

We’ll acknowledge within 1–2 business days with RMA instructions. Once received, typical triage is within 48 hours.

6) DOA (Dead on Arrival)

If your device appears faulty within 14 days of delivery, contact us immediately. We may request evidence and will advise next steps. If confirmed, we will repair, replace, or refund at our discretion.

7) Returns (Change of Mind)

For refurbished electronics, change-of-mind returns are not accepted. This does not affect your statutory rights.

8) Transport & Risk

Use tracked shipping and adequate packaging. Risk in transit lies with the sender until the device is received by Shadow. For approved warranty repairs, we will return ship using tracked services.

9) Limits & Liability

The remedies in this Policy are your sole and exclusive remedies. To the maximum extent permitted by law, we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss (including loss of data, revenue, or profit). Our aggregate liability shall not exceed the price paid for the product or repair at issue.

10) Privacy

Personal data provided in connection with a warranty or repair is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
