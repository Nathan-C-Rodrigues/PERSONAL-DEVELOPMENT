Warranty & Returns

  • Refurbished devices include a 6‑month hardware warranty covering defects in parts or workmanship.
  • Repairs include a 90‑day parts & labour warranty (excludes liquid damage and accidental damage).
  • No returns are accepted unless the fault is directly attributable to Shadow Technologies’ supplied device or workmanship.
  • Accidental damage, misuse, or liquid damage is not covered under warranty.
  • Warranty is void if Samsung Knox has been tripped, or if the device has been rooted/modified.
  • Warranty is void if the device has been opened, altered, or repaired by any third party outside Shadow Technologies.
