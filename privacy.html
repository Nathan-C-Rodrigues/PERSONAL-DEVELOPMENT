3) Why we use your data (lawful bases)

  • Contract — to process orders, repairs, collections/returns, warranties, and customer support.
  • Legal obligation — tax, invoices, accounting records, product safety.
  • Legitimate interests — fraud prevention, service quality, basic site security and logs.
  • Consent — optional updates/marketing (only if you opt in; you can withdraw any time).

4) Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies only for basic site functions (e.g., remembering form progress). We do not run advertising trackers. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and request consent where required.

5) Sharing your data

We share only what is necessary with:

  • Payment processor (e.g., Revolut) to take payment securely.
  • Couriers/logistics to deliver or collect devices.
  • Service tools for forms and email (e.g., Formspree, email provider).
  • Professional advisors (accountants, auditors, insurers) for compliance.
  • Law enforcement/regulators where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

6) International transfers

Our providers may process data in the EEA/UK or, if outside, under GDPR-approved safeguards (e.g., EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

7) Retention

  • Orders, invoices, warranty/repair records: typically 6–7 years (tax/accounting).
  • Routine correspondence and diagnostics: kept only as long as needed to resolve the case.
  • Marketing (opt-in): until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent.
  • Server logs: short, security-focused retention.

8) Your rights (GDPR)

You can request: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email contact@shadowtechnologies.eu. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission).

9) Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures: restricted access to service data, encrypted transport, ESD-safe handling, and controlled workflows. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and devices.

10) Children

Our site and services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is posted here with the “Last updated” date.

12) Contact

For privacy queries or requests, contact: contact@shadowtechnologies.eu.
