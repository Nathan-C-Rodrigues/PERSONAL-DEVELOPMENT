Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2025

Shadow Technologies Ltd (“Shadow”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you contact us, place an order, or request a repair through shadowtechnologies.eu.

1) What we collect

We only collect the information you deliberately provide — for example, when:

  • you place an order (name, address, email, phone, product details),
  • you book a repair (device model, fault description, contact email),
  • you email us directly.

We do not use analytics, advertisements, or tracking technologies. No cookies are stored on your device by this website.

2) How we use your information

  • To process your order or repair request and communicate with you.
  • To maintain records for warranty, invoicing, and accounting.
  • To comply with Irish and EU tax or legal obligations.

3) What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or tracking pixels.
  • We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly ask to receive updates.

4) Sharing and storage

Data you provide may appear in:

  • our payment processor (Revolut Business) for order payments,
  • our courier partners for shipping or returns,
  • our email system to reply to your inquiries.

All such processing is limited to what’s required to fulfill your request.

5) Retention

Order and invoice data are kept for legal accounting purposes (typically 6–7 years). General correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your inquiry.

6) Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access, correction, or deletion of your data, or restrict its use. To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@shadowtechnologies.eu.

7) Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restrict internal access to customer information. No cloud analytics or third-party advertising systems are integrated with our site.

8) Children

Our site and services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
