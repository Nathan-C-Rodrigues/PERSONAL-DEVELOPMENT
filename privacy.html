Privacy Policy

  • Basic analytics/cookies to improve the site (if enabled).

No customer accounts are created. We collect only what is needed to process your order or repair and to meet legal obligations.

How we use it

Sharing

We may share necessary data with service providers (e.g., payment processors, couriers, email tools) strictly to deliver our services. We do not sell your data.

Retention

We keep records only as long as needed for the purposes above and legal requirements.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact: contact@shadowtechnologies.eu.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies for basic functionality. If we add analytics, we will update this policy and request consent where required.
